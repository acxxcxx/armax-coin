## 1. Executive Summary

Armaxcoin v1.0.10 upgrades the consensus difficulty adjustment mechanism on the testnet from LWMA-1 to the pure integer **Armax-ASERT-AW** algorithm at **Block `#13,335`**, resolving long-standing moving-average oscillation, multi-block lag, and catastrophic post-GPU difficulty lock-in.

In addition to the consensus DAA hard fork, v1.0.10 delivers comprehensive non-consensus hardening for miners and nodes:
1. **Thread-Safe `MiningJob` Engine:** Eliminates goroutine race conditions and state tearing across the 1,200s TEDR boundary with immutable snapshots and a 4-point submission gate.
2. **P2P Clock-Skew Resilience:** Typed `*core.FutureTimestampError` reporting exact skew, negative cache bypass for future blocks, and a 3-tier P2P scoring policy.
3. **True Parent-Context Orphan Validation:** Reconstructs parent-branch expected targets instead of evaluating orphans against the active chain tip.
4. **Surge Cap & TEDR Boost:** Limits single-block drift injection to 4 hours (~15.63x drop per block) while enabling rapid 60x difficulty relaxation across 3 emergency blocks under TEDR.

## 2. Detailed Technical Specifications

### 2.1 Armax-ASERT-AW Consensus Engine (`core/asert.go`)
- **Canonical Math:** Implements pure integer `aserti3-2d` algorithm:
  $$\text{exponent} = \text{FloorDiv}(\text{driftSeconds} \cdot 65536, \tau)$$
  $$\text{shifts} = \text{exponent} \gg 16$$
  $$\text{frac} = \text{exponent} \ \& \ 0\text{xFFFF}$$
  $$\text{factor} = 65536 + \left\lfloor \frac{195766423245049 \cdot \text{frac} + 971821376 \cdot \text{frac}^2 + 5127 \cdot \text{frac}^3 + 2^{47}}{2^{48}} \right\rfloor$$
  $$\text{target} = \frac{\text{anchorTarget} \cdot \text{factor}}{65536} \cdot 2^{\text{shifts}}$$
- **Half-Life:** $\tau = 3600\text{s}$ (30 blocks).
- **Exact Test Vectors:** Validated bit-for-bit against all 17 canonical integer test vectors.

### 2.2 Anchor Resolution at Block `#13,334` (`ResolveASERTAnchor`)
- Anchor Height: $h_{\text{anchor}} = H_{\text{act}} - 1 = 13,334$.
- Anchor Timestamp: $t_{\text{anchor}} = B_{13334}.\text{Timestamp}$.
- **Anti-Poisoning Guard (DA-03):** If Block `#13,334` was an emergency block (`IsBlockEmergencyRecord`), the node scans backwards to select the last legitimate normal target as $T_{\text{anchor}}$, preventing emergency difficulty contamination.
- **Continuity Guarantee:** At Block `#13,335`, relative drift $e = 0 \implies T_{13335} = T_{\text{anchor}}$, providing zero difficulty reset and zero step discontinuity.

### 2.3 Surge Protection & TEDR Boost
- **4-Hour Surge Cap (`MaxASERTBlockInterval = 14400s`):** Clamps single-block solve time $\Delta t$ to 14,400s, capping single-block target relaxation to $\approx 15.63\times$ and limiting post-outage block rush to at most 120 blocks.
- **TEDR Boost (`TEDRBoostDriftSeconds = 7200s`):** When an emergency block is mined under TEDR ($\Delta t > 1,200\text{s}$), synthetic drift of $7,200\text{s} - 120\text{s} = 7,080\text{s}$ is injected, lowering subsequent normal difficulty by $\approx 3.91\times$ per emergency block.
- **200x Hashrate Crash Recovery:** Recovers network target by $\approx 60\times$ within 3 emergency blocks, allowing remaining CPU miners to resume regular 120s block intervals.

### 2.4 Immutable Thread-Safe `MiningJob` Engine (`core/blockchain.go`, `miner/miner.go`)
- **Worker Isolation:** Replaces mutable header references with immutable `MiningJob` structs.
- **Atomic Rotation:** Thread-safe job rotation increments `JobVersion` atomically upon target changes across the 1,200s TEDR window.
- **4-Point Submission Gate:**
  1. Job active check: Candidate job version must match current `JobVersion`.
  2. Header consistency: `Header.Target` and `Header.Difficulty` must match job snapshot.
  3. Timestamp window: `Job.Timestamp <= Header.Timestamp <= Job.MaxTime`.
  4. PoW threshold: Header digest must strictly meet `Job.TargetBytes`.

### 2.5 Clock-Skew Resilience & 3-Tier P2P Policy (`core/timestamp_rules.go`, `node/p2p.go`)
- Concrete error type `*core.FutureTimestampError` reporting exact `SkewSeconds`.
- Negative cache bypass for future timestamp errors: blocks are retried and accepted once wall-clock time catches up.
- 3-Tier P2P scoring:
  - $\le 30\text{s}$ skew: 0 ban score.
  - $31\text{s} - 600\text{s}$ skew: Transient strike recorded; ban penalty only after 3 repeated strikes in 10 minutes.
  - $> 600\text{s}$ skew: +10 ban score.

### 2.6 Dual-Model Difficulty Display & Hashrate Telemetry (`core/target.go`, `node/server.go`, `gui/main.go`, `miner/miner.go`)
- **Dual-Model Difficulty Metrics:** Both absolute work (`hashes_per_block`, formatted as hashes/solves) and relative difficulty ratio (`difficulty_ratio`, relative to PoW limit) are exported across RPC endpoints (`/stats`, `/getmininginfo`, `/getblocktemplate`), GUI dashboards, and miner logs.
- **Dynamic DAA Engine Labels:** Explicitly distinguishes active DAA engine (`ASERT` vs `LWMA-1`) and displays ASERT schedule drift (e.g. `+12.4s ahead` or `-45.2s behind`).

### 2.7 P2P Stale Peer Pruning & Reachability Cooldown (`node/p2p.go`, `node/dht.go`)
- **2-Hour Inactivity Rule (`MaxPeerInactivityDuration = 2h`):** Automatically detects and evicts non-seed public peers that have failed dials and remained unresponsive for $\ge 2$ hours.
- **Clean DHT & Address Book Purge:** Dead peers are removed from `triedPeers`, `newPeers`, on-disk `peers.json`, and the Kademlia DHT routing table (`RoutingTable.Remove`).
- **Suppression of Stale Peer Gossip (`case "GET_PEERS"`):** Outgoing peer exchange messages filter out stale or unreachable peers, preventing circulating dead IPs through the network.
- **Sync Worker Dial Loop Fix (`SyncBestPeer`):** Skips unreachable and stale peers before initiating connection queries, completely eliminating 15-second dial timeout/refused spam (`[SYNC] GET_HEIGHT failed`).

### 2.8 Misbehavior Ban Duration Reduction (`node/p2p.go`)
- **12-Hour Ban Window (`DefaultBanDuration = 12h`):** Reduced the default IP ban duration from 24 hours to 12 hours for abusive traffic, excessive clock skew strikes, and protocol violations.
